Claim capsule

  • One-dimensional Gaussian kernel: mass, semigroup, exact Lq norms, derivatives and L1 increments.
  • Gaussian-tempered positive measures: convergent heat flow, fixed-time spatial continuity and weighted bounds.
  • Signed Jordan-pair data: total-variation domination, finite SignedMeasure interoperability and nontrivial examples.
  • Chen–Dalang, squared-kernel, heat-flow semigroup and doubled-profile convolution estimates.

Not established by this library

  • Joint time-space heat-flow continuity and weak convergence to the initial datum.
  • Higher dimensions, higher derivatives, Fourier estimates and stochastic integration.
  • Stochastic PDE well-posedness or a general converse from rough measures to finite SignedMeasure.
