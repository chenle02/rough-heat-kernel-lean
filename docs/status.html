Claim capsule

  • One-dimensional Gaussian kernel: mass, semigroup, exact Lq norms, derivatives and L1 increments.
  • Gaussian-tempered positive measures: convergent heat flow, joint positive-time continuity and weighted bounds.
  • Signed Jordan-pair data: total-variation domination, finite SignedMeasure interoperability and nontrivial examples.
  • Chen–Dalang, squared-kernel, heat-flow semigroup and doubled-profile convolution estimates, including the shifted semigroup identity, the pointwise-in-time Chen–Dalang step and a Bochner-to-lower-integral bound.
  • Special functions: real Beta integral on [0, t], diffusion-scale kernel identities, error function, standard normal density and distribution function, and the Gamma series for ez²(1 + erf z).

Not established by this library

  • Continuity at time zero and weak convergence to the initial datum.
  • Higher dimensions, higher derivatives, Fourier estimates and stochastic integration.
  • Stochastic PDE well-posedness or a general converse from rough measures to finite SignedMeasure.
