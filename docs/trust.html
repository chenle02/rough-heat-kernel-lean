Trust and reproducibility

The permitted foundational axioms are Classical.choice, Quot.sound and propext. A theorem remains conditional on every hypothesis in its Lean type.

Different checks establish different facts

  • A clean package build elaborates all selected source modules against the pinned dependencies.
  • Compiled-environment ownership audits include private helpers, definitions, instances and generated declarations; public theorem counts alone do not establish this coverage.
  • Strict negative fixtures must fail for the intended single False goal, after their positive witnesses compile.
  • Native kernel replay checks compiled proof modules. Independent statement comparison checks the configured claimed results against their reference statements.
  • Receipts must identify the actual scientific commit, inputs and retained sealed outputs. Historical evidence cannot certify changed scientific source.

Read the domain

Analytic estimates state positive-time hypotheses. heatKernel has value zero at nonpositive time. heatFlow is a real Bochner integral; rough-data theorems prove the required integrability. lheatFlow is an extended-real lower integral and may be infinite. Signed data are represented by mutually singular Gaussian-tempered Jordan parts, with no finite-total-mass assumption unless explicitly stated.

Not established by this library

  • Continuity at time zero and weak convergence to the initial datum.
  • Higher dimensions, higher derivatives, Fourier estimates and stochastic integration.
  • Stochastic PDE well-posedness or a general converse from rough measures to finite SignedMeasure.
